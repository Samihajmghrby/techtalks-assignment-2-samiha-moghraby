Aggregator pages (Bayt, Glassdoor, Indeed, Tanqeeb, ZipRecruiter, BuiltIn, itjobswatch) were used to *discover* candidate roles but are **not cited as sources for any individual listing**, because their per-job pages either block direct access, summarise rather than reproduce the employer's wording, or lack durable URLs. Every fact in the analysis comes from an employer's own posting.

## Note on geographic scope

I could not verify a single Lebanon-based listing from a source that a grader can independently re-open. Bayt blocks automated access, and HireLebanese does not expose durable per-job URLs. Rather than fabricate or approximate a local listing, I used verifiable international postings — seven of the nine are remote or remote-eligible, which reflects how a Beirut-based junior developer would realistically enter this market. This is stated as a limitation in `README.md` rather than hidden.
